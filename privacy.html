Privacy

What we collect

When you submit a quote request through our website, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your company name

Who sees it

This information is sent to Shipfront staff so we can reply to your inquiry about warehousing and fulfillment services. We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

How to delete it

If you would like us to delete your information, contact us at info@myshipfront.com

What we don't do

  • We do not use tracking or analytics SDKs on this website
  • We do not require account creation or login portals
  • We do not collect information beyond what you submit in the quote form
